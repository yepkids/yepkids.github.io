    How this forecast works

    Each completed 06–06 UTC cycle is paired with the previous calendar date’s ECMWF 12 UTC initialization. The newest available 12 UTC run forecasts the following 06–06 UTC cycle. The 134-cycle history contains 120 training cycles followed by 14 test cycles. Forest inputs include ECMWF minimum and maximum temperature, precipitation sum, maximum wind gust, minimum and maximum relative humidity, mean cloud cover, mean CAPE, dominant wind direction, and maximum wind speed. All inputs must be complete for a training row.

    Four separate random forests predict observed-minus-ECMWF error for minimum temperature, maximum temperature, precipitation, and maximum reported wind gust. Each has 100 trees, maximum depth 4, minimum 5 samples per leaf, and square-root feature selection at every split. Wind direction is encoded as sine and cosine. Predictions add the forest’s estimated error to the raw ECMWF value. Negative precipitation and gust forecasts are set to zero.

    The last 14 completed cycles are held out for testing. Forests used for testing see only the earlier 120 cycles; the displayed forecast is refitted on the most recent 120 cycles, including the tested dates after evaluation. At least 30 matched training cycles and 5 test cycles are needed to report errors. The forest may perform worse than raw ECMWF; compare the two MAE columns.

    After training, this browser keeps the four station forests and their test results for 30 days. Each new forecast requests the latest available ECMWF 12 UTC run and applies those saved forests. The historical run cache is separate and lasts seven days. Clearing browser site data, using a different browser, or reaching the 30-day expiry requires training again.

    METAR observations are grouped into 06–06 UTC windows. We require reports in at least 18 distinct hours per target. Reported temperatures and gusts may miss extremes between METARs; hourly precipitation can straddle the 06 UTC boundary, so these targets approximate official WxChallenge verification. A cycle with no reported gust is treated as zero reported gust. Trace precipitation is treated as zero.

    Forecasts: Open-Meteo Single Runs API (usage terms), ECMWF IFS HRES 9 km. METAR observations and station metadata: Iowa Environmental Mesonet. Forecasts are experimental.
